About me

Learning with purpose.
Building with practice.

An ECE graduate transitioning into software development, with a focus on Java full-stack technologies and practical implementation.

I completed my B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering at Rajiv Gandhi University of Knowledge Technologies, Ongole, with a CGPA of 8.45. I’m now developing my software engineering skills through structured training and project-based learning, building on a two-month startup experience working on products with the PERN stack.

At Vcube Institute, I’ve been studying Java core concepts—including JVM architecture, Collections, Java 8 features, and design patterns—and building POCs with JDBC, multithreading, HTML/CSS/Bootstrap, and problem-solving exercises. I also spent two months contributing to a startup’s e-commerce application, implementing React UI from Figma designs, developing Node.js APIs, integrating them with Axios, and defining the PostgreSQL schema through Prisma ORM. I’m also developing larger applications—RideFlow and an AI Chat Assistant—to apply the concepts I learn in a more realistic context.

I enjoy breaking a topic down, implementing a working example, debugging it, and then improving the design. My current focus is writing clearer code, strengthening DSA, and understanding how backend, database, security, testing, and frontend pieces work together.

What I’m aiming for: an entry-level software development role where I can contribute to a team, keep learning, and grow into a dependable full-stack engineer.
